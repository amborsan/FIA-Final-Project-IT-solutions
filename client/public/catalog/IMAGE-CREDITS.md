# Catalog photo credits

These photos are dedicated to the public domain by their creators under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) or a public-domain
release stated on the linked source page. The local files are resized and
converted to WebP. Selected images are cropped for the catalog layout.
Catalog photos are displayed as illustrative examples where an exact product
image is unavailable.
The `-400.webp` files are smaller versions of their corresponding photos.
